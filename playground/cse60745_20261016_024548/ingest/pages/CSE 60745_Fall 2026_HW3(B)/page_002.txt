Q1: Structural Encoding in Graph Transformer (20 pts)
Complete LapPETransformer() and SPDAttention() to implement graph Laplacian absolute 
structural encoding and shortest path distance-based relative structural encoding in graph 
transformer. You may refer to the demo in Section 4 for implementation ideas.
Q2: Efficient Graph Transformer (20 pts)
Complete SparseSPDAttention() to implement attention mask for sparse graph attention. You only 
need to specify the node pairs used for attention in your implementation. It is encouraged to test 
different masking strategies. 
Q3: Hybrid Graph Transformer (30 pts)
Complete HybridGTLayer() to implement a hybrid graph transformer composed of both MPNN-
based local attention and transformer-based global attention. You can implement GraphGPS or 
design your own hybrid structures.
