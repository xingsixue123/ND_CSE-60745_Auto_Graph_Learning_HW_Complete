In this class, we have two-fold course objectives. The first one is to explore recent core techniques 
and advances in graph learning research, in the forms of class lectures, selected paper presentations 
and discussion, covering the topics of i) graph embedding techniques, ii) graph neural networks, iii) 
knowledge graphs, and iv) advanced topics on graph learning. Through our learning, we want to 
summarize the benchmarks and baselines that have been widely used for graph representation 
learning for 1) homogeneous graphs, 2) heterogeneous graphs; and 3) knowledge graphs.
Problem 1 (35 pts). Based on the above context, you are asked to summarize the benchmarks, 
baselines, and commonly used evaluation metrics for graph embedding methods with homogeneous 
graph structures for the task of node classification and link prediction as comprehensive as you can. 
Note: (1) For the benchmarks, please describe each of the benchmark graphs (e.g., Cora) and draw 
the related graph schema. (2) For the commonly used baselines, please provide the reference (i.e., 
original paper) of each baseline and briefly describe the method.
