2.1 The standardised suite: HGB
Heterogeneous GNN papers before 2021 each preprocessed DBLP/ACM/IMDB their own way, so
their reported numbers were not comparable. HGB (Heterogeneous Graph Benchmark; Lv, Ding,
Liu et al., KDD 2021) exists precisely to fix this: fixed splits, fixed features, one leaderboard. Its
headline statistics are Table 4; the per-type breakdown is Table 5.
