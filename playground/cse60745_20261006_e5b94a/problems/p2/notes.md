## Round 1 correction
My first answer explained the pooled constant's advantage as less smoothing inside the
listening relations. That was wrong; run/ablation.py measures the real cause. Pooling
rescales listens by 1.0000 and listened_by by 0.9785 (untouched) but friend by 0.0451
(22.1x down), and the ablation gives val MRR A per-relation 0.1809, B pooled-all 0.3053,
C friend-pooled-only 0.3253, D listening-pooled-only 0.2465 -- C alone exceeds B. Variant
A is bit-identical to the delivered layer, so the comparison is like for like.
run/leak_check.py then shows why suppressing friend helps: all 19,464 training positives
ARE the relation-0 edges, and none of the val/test positives are.
