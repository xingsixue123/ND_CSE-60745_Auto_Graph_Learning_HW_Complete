Why this is 8 hops, and why n layers=1.
One call to propagate moves information one hop,
so a stack of L layers each propagating K times has a receptive field of L×K hops. This layer runs
K = 8 steps inside a single forward, so 1 × 8 = 8 hops, and the Q5 cell is run with n layers=1.
Because the propagation holds no weights, that depth is free: the model has 8,320 parameters,
identical to the 2-layer GCN and a quarter of the 8-layer GCN’s 33,280. α = 0.3 was chosen on
validation MRR alone and sits on a plateau (val MRR 0.733–0.737 for α ∈[0.25, 0.5], falling to
0.699 at α = 0.2); the original paper uses α = 0.1 with K = 10.
