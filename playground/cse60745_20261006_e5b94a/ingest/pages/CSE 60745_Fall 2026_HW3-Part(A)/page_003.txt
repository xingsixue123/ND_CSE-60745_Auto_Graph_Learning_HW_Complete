Q5: Mitigate Over-Smoothing in Deep GNNs (6 pts)
Stacking too many GNN layers will make all node embeddings drift towards each other and cause 
over-smoothing problem. Please look into the literature on over-smoothing and implement 
MyDeepConv() to design a GNN model that stays useful with an 8-hop receptive field. Note that 8-
hop receptive field is not equal to 8-layer GNN.
