Hands-on: Graph Embedding Over Homogeneous Structures
Recap: Random Walk-Based Graph Embedding
•
DeepWalk: Uniform random walk sampling + Skip-gram contextualized embedding
•
Node2vec: Biased random walk sampling + Skip-gram contextualized embedding
•
Struct2vec: Structural similarity modeling + Multi-layer random walk
