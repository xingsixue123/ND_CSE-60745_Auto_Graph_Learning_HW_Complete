The graph.

What is reused unchanged.
This is what makes the comparison in Table 4 legitimate, so I
state it precisely. train embedding, LinkPredictor and CountryClassifier are the same Python
objects measured in Task 3: my sampler is a subclass of Task 3’s WalkEmbedding that overrides
sample walks and nothing else, and Task 3’s module is imported byte-identically (verified by

Evaluation is run experiment with Htrain and Nusers+Nartists, slicing Z to [: Nusers]; degree buckets
always come from the social graph Gtrain. The sampler and the graph are the only things

Schema convention.
A schema is a symmetric type string whose repeating cycle is schema[:-1]:
U-A-U gives U A U A . . . , U-U-A-U gives U U A U U A . . . , U-U-U-A-U gives U U U A . . . At each step
the next node is drawn uniformly from the neighbours of the demanded type. Since U-A-U-U is a
cyclic rotation of U-U-A-U and yields the same multiset of step types, I run one schema per rotation
class. The three then form a one-parameter family indexed by the fraction of steps forced to be
social (0, 1/3, 1/2, with Task 3’s DeepWalk as the 1 endpoint), the axis of Figure 4.
Start nodes, and dead ends.
embed graph starts walks from every node of H, artists included.
I rotate the cycle to the start node’s type rather than discarding artist starts, so an artist begins
A U A U . . . — the same meta-path read from the other end; artists thus get trained vectors that
serve as shared context, and are sliced off before evaluation. A walk stops whenever the type the
schema demands next is absent. At the first step this drops the walk entirely (a user with no
artist under U-A-U, 173 of them; a socially isolated user under U-U-A-U/U-U-U-A-U, 666), exactly
as Task 3’s sampler returns [] for an isolated node — so those users keep an all-zero row in both
tasks and the cold-start comparison stays like-for-like. But it also fires mid-walk, overwhelmingly
when an A →U step lands on one of the 666 isolated users and the schema then asks for U →U.
That case is the common one, and it is why walk count and corpus size are different quantities: U-
A-U emits 152,930 walks from 15,293 starts, all of full length 40, whereas U-U-A-U and U-U-U-A-U
emit 148,000 from 14,800 starts with mean length only 24.3 and 27.5 (32.2% and 43.7% reach 40).
Terminating on a dead end is standard metapath2vec behaviour, but Section 2.2 shows its effect
must be measured, not assumed.
