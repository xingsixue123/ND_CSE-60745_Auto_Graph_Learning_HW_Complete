(21 points)
Conventions used throughout.
The statement’s hint says that a miss penalty is an additional
cost on top of the hit time, and that a miss is discovered only after the access to that level has
been attempted. The hierarchy is therefore modelled by the nested expression
AMAT = HTL1 + MRL1 PL1,
PL1 = HTL2 + MRL2 PL2,
PL2 = HTL3 + MRL3 PL3, (1)
with HTL1 = 1, HTL2 = 10, HTL3 = 50 CC, and PL3 the main-memory access cost. The miss
rates supplied in Questions C and D are local miss rates, which is exactly what (1) consumes, so
no conversion to global rates is needed.
