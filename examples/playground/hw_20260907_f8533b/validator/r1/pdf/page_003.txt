Question D (2 points)
The miss rates are unchanged and the initial main memory access time carried forward is the
maximum value found in Question C, M = 305 CC (= 152.5 ns). Only the bus width changes,
from 16 to 32 Bytes, so a 128-Byte block now takes 128/32 = 4 transfers, of which 4 −1 = 3 are
additional. By (2),
PL3 = 305 + 10 × 3 = 335 CC.
Working back up through (1):
PL2 = 50 + 0.20 × 335 = 117 CC,
PL1 = 10 + 0.40 × 117 = 56.8 CC,
AMAT = 1 + 0.05 × 56.8 = 3.84 CC.
The average memory access time will be 3.84 CCs (equivalently 1.92 ns). As expected, doubling
the bus width removes 40 CC of transfer time from the L3 miss penalty and lowers the AMAT
below the 4 CC budget of Question C.
