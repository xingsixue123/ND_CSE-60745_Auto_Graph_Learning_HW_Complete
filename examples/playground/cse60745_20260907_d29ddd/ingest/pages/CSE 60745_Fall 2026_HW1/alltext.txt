Q4: Graph Essentials (30 pts)
(Note: You may use NetworkX to facilitate your solutions.)

number of this component (10 pts).

Q5: Graph Measures (30 pts)
(Note: You may use NetworkX to facilitate your solutions.)

default parameters in NetworkX)
Report average clustering coefficient of all nodes in the graph, report average Jaccard similarity of 
all connected node pairs in the graph (10 pts).
